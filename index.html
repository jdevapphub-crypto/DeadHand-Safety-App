DEAD HAND
by Joheliv Labs

DEAD HAND is a personal safety application that automatically notifies your trusted contacts if you don't check in — and helps small groups stay connected through live location while they travel together.

🛡️ Safety Mode

Set a check-in timer from 1 minute to 24 hours. If you don't check in before it runs out, DEAD HAND sends your last known GPS location to your emergency contacts by SMS and Email.

👥 Group Safety Mode

Invite 2–4 friends to a shared group using a code. Everyone sees each other live on a map, and if anyone drifts more than 100 metres away, an alert with a live map link is sent automatically.

📜 Will Mode

For long-term situations — months or years. Your final message, land papers, or last words are stored and delivered automatically to up to 7 recipients if you never check back in.

📜 PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026

Joheliv Labs ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data DEAD HAND collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. What We Collect

  • GPS location — used to include a live map link in emergency alerts and to show group members on the live map while Group Mode is active.
  • Contact details you provide — names, phone numbers, and email addresses of your chosen emergency contacts and Will recipients.
  • Your message content — the safety note or Will message you write for delivery if a timer triggers.
  • Device identifier — a random ID stored in your browser to keep your session consistent. Not linked to your real identity.
  • Delivery metadata — timestamps and success/failure status of SMS/Email alerts, so you can see what was sent.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To send alerts to your chosen contacts if you don't check in.
  • To display live group positions while Group Mode is running.
  • To provide a delivery log so you can audit what happened.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (operated by Google LLC). Location data is shared in real time with other members of your group only while Group Mode is active.

4. Who Sees Your Data

  • Your emergency contacts / Will recipients — receive the message and location you set.
  • Your group members — see your live location only while Group Mode is active.
  • Delivery providers — BulkSMS (SMS) and Resend (Email) receive only the phone number/email and message content required to deliver.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers.

5. Location Data

Location is only accessed while the app is open and you have granted permission. Group Mode stops transmitting when you tap "Stop Group Tracking" or close the app. Browsers may throttle background updates — the app works best while visible on screen.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data (device ID, active timer, last location) is stored in your browser and cleared when you tap "Disarm" or clear your browser data.
  • Server-side data (users, group members, delivery events) is retained to support the service. You can request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing deadhandjoheliv@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

DEAD HAND is not intended for users under 16. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all traffic. API credentials for SMS and Email providers are stored server-side and never exposed to other users.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes. Continued use after changes means you accept the new policy.
